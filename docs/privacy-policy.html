Privacy Policy

Your files never leave your phone.

EFFECTIVE DATE: 29 OCTOBER 2026 · APP: STORAGEPULSE AI · PACKAGE: com.storagepulse.cleaner

Short version. StoragePulse AI scans, maps, de-duplicates, compresses and encrypts your files entirely on your device. Your photos, videos, documents, file names, folder structure, image fingerprints (perceptual hashes) and app-usage information are processed in your phone's memory and its private app storage, and are never transmitted to us or to any server. We do not run accounts, cloud backups or our own analytics.

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device and advertising data (section 5). The optional one-time Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play Billing (section 6).

1. Who we are

StoragePulse AI ("the app", "we", "us") is published on Google Play by an independent developer. Contact: skkhatri2024@gmail.com.

2. What the app works with on your device

To do its job, the app reads information that stays on your phone:

  • Files and media on shared storage (photos, videos, audio, documents, APK files, WhatsApp / Telegram media folders): their names, paths, sizes and dates, and for images small thumbnails, to build the storage map, find duplicates and blurry shots, and offer clean-up.
  • Image fingerprints: a 64-bit perceptual hash (dHash), a sharpness score and, for files of identical size, a SHA-256 checksum. These are cached in the app's private database on your device so re-analysis is fast.
  • App usage and app sizes (only if you allow Usage Access): when each installed app was last opened and how much storage it uses, to list apps you have not opened for 30+ days.
  • List of launchable installed apps, to show their sizes. We do not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.

All of this is processed locally, in device memory and app-private storage. None of it is sent off the device, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

4. Data stored on your device

  • Safe Trash: files you "delete" are moved to a hidden .StoragePulseTrash folder on the same storage and kept restorable for 30 days (7, 30 or 60 days in Settings), then permanently deleted on the device. "Empty Trash" deletes them immediately after you confirm.
  • Private Vault: files you move into the vault are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in the Android Keystore and stored in the app's private folder. The vault is removed if you uninstall the app, so restore files first.
  • Settings and caches (access mode, retention period, accent colour, image fingerprints, widget numbers, Pro status) stay in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup.
