Data safety

What leaves your phone (and what never does)

MATCHES THE GOOGLE PLAY DATA SAFETY SECTION · UPDATED 29 OCTOBER 2026

Never leaves your device

Photos, videos, audio, documents, file names, folder structure, image fingerprints, app-usage history, installed-app list, vault contents. Processed on-device only.

Collected by Google AdMob (free version)

Advertising / device IDs, approximate location from IP, ad interactions, SDK diagnostics. Used for ads, measurement and fraud prevention.

Handled by Google Play

The one-time Pro purchase. We only receive a purchase token to unlock Pro; no payment details.

Security practices

  • Encrypted in transit: yes. AdMob and Google Play traffic uses TLS.
  • You can request deletion: nothing is stored on our servers. On-device data is deleted by clearing app storage or uninstalling. Advertising data can be reset in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Independent security review: not performed.
  • Families policy: not designed for children.
